About TBanking

TBanking is a lightweight banking front-end prototype inspired by modern mobile money apps. It demonstrates sending money, paying bills, topping up airtime/data, and a simple wallet dashboard — all designed to be responsive and accessible.

Features

Send Money

Transfer funds instantly to anyone, anywhere.

Pay Bills

Settle your utility bills and subscriptions in seconds.

Airtime & Data

Top up your phone or buy data bundles anytime.

Wallet

Manage your digital wallet and track your spending.
